```text

Crisp macro shot of clean hands gently harvesting the wild pod in Kilifi. Cut to clean stainless laboratory scale measuring the raw pulp against fresh oranges.

PROMPT (Scene 3 - 15 to 28s - The Evidence & Laboratory Check):
Clean laboratory bench shot with amber glassware and HPLC testing readouts. Graphic animation of pectin fibers moving into the digestive tract.
On-screen text: "Grade A Clinical Evidence: 100% Soluble Dietary Prebiotic Fibre."

PROMPT (Scene 4 - 29 to 45s - The Daily Ritual & Call to Action):
Smooth slow-motion pour of cool water into a minimalist glass tumbler. One teaspoon of NewAge Pure Baobab Powder swirls into a refreshing, silky citrus elixir. A hand lifts the glass. Cut to the NewAge Botanicals luxury pouch with QR code in focus.
```
