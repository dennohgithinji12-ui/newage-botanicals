Treating **NewAge Botanicals** as a sustainable consumer brand requires a customer acquisition engine built on **empirical evidence, transparency, cultural resonance, and tight unit economics**.

---

## 1. Commercial Unit Economics & Guardrails
Based on the **Gate 6 Costing Engine** results:
- **Average Catalog Gross Margin:** **78.7%**
- **Average Net Contribution Margin:** **69.4%** (KES 1,024 per unit on standard KES 1,500 retail)
- **Target Blended CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** **KES 520** (Ceiling: KES 840)
- **Target Initial AOV (Average Order Value):** **KES 2,850** (~2 products or 1 ritual bundle)
- **Target 90-Day LTV (Lifetime Value):** **KES 7,200** (2.5 orders per active customer)
- **Target LTV:CAC Ratio:** **≥ 4.0:1**

### Channel 1: Organic Botanical Atlas & Programmatic SEO
- **Strategy:** Position NewAge Botanicals as the definitive, clinical-grade encyclopedia for African and global herbal medicine.
- **Content Hubs:**
  1. *Botanical Dossiers* (Taxonomy, Phytochemicals, Clinical Trials vs Traditional Use).
  2. *Symptom Matcher* ("Natural remedies for post-meal bloating in Kenya", "Herbal adaptogens for work burnout").
  3. *Comparative Guides* ("Moringa vs Green Tea for morning energy", "East African Nilotica Shea vs West African Shea for eczema").
- **Conversion Mechanism:** Every educational article features embedded product cards with one-click Paystack checkout and direct batch CoA inspection.

### Channel 2: High-Velocity Short-Form Video (Google Flow Pipeline)
- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts.
- **Cadence:** 1 video per day (30-day continuous cycle).
- **Core Video Formats:**
  1. **"Plant of the Week" (45s):** Fast visual narrative of a single botanical, its indigenous harvesting in Kenya, and its primary active molecule.
  2. **"Myth vs Clinical Evidence" (60s):** Busting wild internet health claims while revealing verified pharmacological research.
  3. **"Soil to Self: Traceable Journey" (50s):** Visual journey showing wild harvesting in Nanyuki, Kitui, or Kilifi, followed by accredited laboratory ICP-MS testing.
  4. **"Phytochemical Deep Dive" (40s):** Microscopic/3D animations showing how bioactive markers (curcuminoids, withanolides, oleic acid) interact with human biology.

### Channel 3: Practitioner & Micro-Creator Seeding Network
- **Target Cohort:** 50 selected Kenyan and diaspora creators:
  - 15 Functional Nutritionists & Naturopathic Doctors.
  - 15 Yoga & Somatic Wellness Instructors.
  - 10 Clean Beauty Estheticians & Dermatological Advocates.
  - 10 High-Altitude Kenyan Runners & Endurance Athletes.
- **Seed Kit:** Custom unbleached wooden box with 2 hero remedies, laser-engraved bamboo dosing spoon, printed Certificate of Analysis, and QR code to their personalized affiliate portal (12% commission).

### Channel 4: WhatsApp Conversational Commerce (`+254700123456`)
- **Strategy:** In East Africa, conversational commerce converts 3.4x higher than standard web checkout.
- **Click-to-WhatsApp Ads:** Ads prompt users: *"Chat with our Master Herbal Alchemist on WhatsApp for a personalized ritual recommendation."*
- **Automated Workflow:**
  1. Greeting & Symptom Assessment (Sleep, Digestion, Energy, Hair/Skin, Vitality).
  2. Interactive product recommendation with pricing in KES.
  3. Direct Paystack M-Pesa Express payment link generated in chat.
  4. Instant order confirmation and real-time courier tracking.

### Channel 5: Hyper-Targeted Performance Paid Ads (Meta & Google)
- **Objective:** Scale customer acquisition while strictly preserving the KES 520 CAC target.

#### Ad Campaign Architecture:
1. **TOFU (Top of Funnel — Cold Traffic):**
   - *Angle A (Scientific Curiosity):* "Why Most Moringa and Turmeric in Kenya Fails Lab Testing (And How to Check Your Batch CoA)."
   - *Angle B (Problem/Ailment):* "Waking Up at 3:00 AM with Racing Thoughts? Why African Nervine Herbs Calm Evening Cortisol."
   - *Angle C (Heritage Luxury):* "Wild Nilotica Shea: The Silky East African Skin Secret Dermatologists Love."
2. **MOFU (Middle of Funnel — High Consideration):**
   - Video social proof: Real customer unboxings, laboratory assay reviews, and 30-day before/after routines.
   - Retargeting visitors who engaged with the Discovery Hub or AI Herbalist.
3. **BOFU (Bottom of Funnel — Direct Conversion):**
   - "First 100 Founding Members" collector's offer with free domestic courier dispatch over KES 5,000 and M-Pesa Express checkout.
