Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order and pay with M-Pesa or Card?
Simply add your chosen herbal formulations to your basket, click "Pay with Paystack", and enter your delivery details. You can pay instantly using M-Pesa (by entering your Safaricom phone number to trigger an STK push) or with any Visa, Mastercard, Verve, or Apple Pay. You receive an instant digital receipt and WhatsApp dispatch notification.
Where do you ship, and how fast is delivery across Kenya?
We deliver across Nairobi and all major towns in Kenya (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret) via express courier, typically arriving within 24–48 hours. Orders over KSh 4,500 enjoy free standard delivery. Worldwide international shipping is also available via DHL Express.
How are NewAge Health botanicals harvested and crafted?
All our botanicals are ethically wildcrafted and small-batch processed across Kenya's rich microclimates—from Mount Kenya's volcanic slopes to Kilifi's ancient baobab groves. We utilize gentle solar curing and cold extraction to preserve vital enzymatic life-force without synthetic preservatives or chemical fillers.
Are these formulations safe to combine, or to use alongside other supplements?
Our daily nutritional powders (Moringa, Baobab) and gentle functional herbal teas (Hibiscus, Chamomile, Peppermint) are whole-food botanicals safe for regular wellness integration. For concentrated therapeutic tinctures or during pregnancy/nursing, we recommend consulting our AI Herbalist or your healthcare practitioner.
Is Apple Pay supported for mobile checkout?
Yes! Our domain omnicorehub.app is officially registered and verified with Apple Pay through Paystack. When shopping on Safari or iOS devices, Apple Pay will appear automatically at checkout for one-touch biometric payment.
