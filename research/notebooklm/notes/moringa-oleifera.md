# BOTANICAL DOSSIER: Moringa oleifera

## TAXONOMY & IDENTIFICATION
- Common Name: Moringa / Miracle Tree / Drumstick Tree
- Scientific Binomial: Moringa oleifera Lam.
- Family: Moringaceae
- Indigenous East African Names: Mlonge / Mzungwi (Swahili), Mururue (Meru), Muzungu (Kamba)
- Plant Part Examined: Leaves (Dried, powdered)

## PHYTOCHEMICAL PROFILE
- Primary Active Constituents: Quercetin-3-O-glucoside, Chlorogenic acid, Kaempferol, Isothiocyanates (Moringin), beta-carotene, Vitamin C, zeatin, 9 essential amino acids
- Standardization Markers: Total polyphenols (minimum 4.2% w/w), total flavonoids (minimum 1.8% w/w)

## EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE & CLINICAL DATA
- Evidence Level: GRADE_A
- Human Clinical Findings: Systematic reviews and randomized controlled trials confirm postprandial glucose reduction (mean 13.5-19% reduction in impaired fasting glucose cohorts), lipid peroxide reduction, and significant elevation of plasma antioxidant status (malondialdehyde reduction). 
- Mechanistic Action: Moringin isothiocyanates activate Nrf2 antioxidant response elements, downregulating NF-kB inflammatory cascades and improving insulin receptor substrate phosphorylation.

## SAFETY, TOXICOLOGY & CONTRAINDICATIONS
- Safety Rating: Safe for daily dietary use
- Known Contraindications: Mild caution in early pregnancy due to potential uterine contraction markers in bark/root (leaves demonstrate wide safety margin, but conservative labeling advised).
- Drug-Herb Interactions: May potentiate prescription hypoglycemic agents and antihypertensive medications. Safe with standard multivitamin supplementation.
- Daily Safe Dosage Range: 3g to 7g dried leaf powder daily.

## TRADITIONAL ETHNOBOTANICAL USE
- Traditional Cultural Context: East African coastal and dryland communities use fresh and dried leaves in broths and daily porridges to restore stamina, nourish postpartum mothers, and alleviate joint fatigue.
- Traditional Claims: Said to cure 300 ailments, purify blood, restore youth, and heal diabetes.

## CRITICAL DISTINCTION: TRADITION VS EVIDENCE
- Validated Traditional Uses: Sustained daily energy, cellular antioxidant protection, modest blood sugar balance, micronutrient replenishment (iron, calcium, magnesium).
- Unverified Folklore Claims: Direct "cure" for type-1 diabetes, cancer cure, instantaneous arthritis eradication.
- Safety Warnings for Marketing: Cannot claim to replace medical insulin or antihypertensives. Must state as dietary botanical support for cellular vitality and metabolic wellness.

## PROCESSING & QUALITY SPECIFICATIONS
- Optimal Drying / Extraction: Gentle shade-drying below 40°C in solar-tunnel dryers to prevent chlorophyll oxidation and heat degradation of ascorbic acid and isothiocyanates. Stone-milled to 100 mesh.
- Common Adulterants & Quality Risks: Sand/silica from outdoor floor drying, high microbial plate counts (aerobic bacteria, E. coli), blending with low-nutrient Leucaena leucocephala leaves.
- Storage & Shelf-Life Stability: Highly hygroscopic. Requires airtight moisture-barrier aluminum-kraft pouches with nitrogen flush or desiccant pack. 24 months stability away from direct sunlight.
